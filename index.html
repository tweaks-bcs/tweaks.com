Academic Results & Achievements

Our students consistently excel in national examinations

🏅 2025 UACE Highlights

Outstanding Performance: Our school achieved remarkable results with multiple AAA scores in sciences, maintaining our position as one of Uganda's leading secondary institutions. Students demonstrated exceptional mastery in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics combinations. Several of our graduates earned government sponsorships to prestigious public universities including Makerere University's Faculty of Technology and Kyambogo University's Engineering programs. The 2025 cohort broke our previous records with 92% of students achieving university-admission grades.

📈 2024 UACE Ranking

National Recognition: Ranked among the top 15 performing schools nationally, we continue to set benchmarks for educational excellence. Our science stream achieved an average score of 18.5 points, significantly above the national average. The strong showing in PCM, PEM, and HEG combinations reflects our rigorous curriculum and dedicated teaching staff. Our A-Level students' success rate for university admission stands at 88%, with graduates attending institutions across East Africa and beyond.

🎓 University Placement

Global Opportunities: Our graduates are placed at leading universities including Makerere University (Faculty of Science, Technology & Engineering), Kyambogo University, Gulu University, Busitema University, Uganda Christian University, and international institutions in Kenya, Tanzania, and Rwanda. We proudly report that our alumni hold positions in government, academia, healthcare, engineering, and corporate sectors, contributing significantly to Uganda's development agenda.

Academic Programmes

Comprehensive O-Level and A-Level curricula with modern facilities

📚 Curriculum Overview

Bukedea Comprehensive School offers a comprehensive, internationally-aligned curriculum designed to foster critical thinking, creativity, and practical skills. Our programs are structured to meet Uganda's national standards while preparing students for global competitiveness.

O-Level (S.1-S.4)

Core subjects: English, Mathematics, Integrated Science, Social Studies. Electives: Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Geography, History, Computer Science, Entrepreneurship, Technical Drawing, French, and Home Economics.

A-Level (S.5-S.6)

Science Stream: Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics. Arts Stream: English, History, Geography, Economics. Technical Stream: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Computer Science.

Admissions

Join a community of excellence — here's how to apply

📋 Requirements

  • PLE results slip (for S.1 applicants)
  • UCE results slip (for S.5 applicants)
  • Recent passport-size photo (3x4)
  • Letter of recommendation (transfers)
  • Birth certificate copy
  • Medical report (optional)
  • Character testimonial from previous school

💳 Fees & Payment

Fees structures vary by campus, stream, and boarding option (Day/Boarding). We offer flexible payment plans through three installments per term. Payment methods include: Bank deposits to our school account, MTN/Airtel Mobile Money transfers, or in-person payment at the bursar's office during working hours. Early payment attracts a 5% discount.

🎖️ Scholarships

Merit scholarships for top O-Level performers (Division 1-2), sports talent scholarships for exceptional athletes, and needs-based bursaries for deserving students. Contact the admissions office for application deadlines, criteria, and documentation required.
