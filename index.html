Academic Results & Achievements

🏅 2025 UACE Highlights

Outstanding performance with multiple AAA scores. Strong showing in PCM, PEM, and HEG combinations. Several students earned government sponsorships to public universities.

Ranked among the top-performing schools nationally. Exceptional results in science combinations with a high university admission rate for our candidates.

Graduates placed at Makerere University, Kyambogo University, Gulu University, Busitema University, and other leading institutions across East Africa.

Admissions

Join a community of excellence — here's how to apply

📋 Requirements

  • PLE results slip (for S.1 applicants)
  • UCE results slip (for S.5 applicants)
  • Recent passport-size photo
  • Letter of recommendation (transfers)
  • Birth certificate copy

💳 Fees & Payment

Fees structures vary by campus and programme. Contact the Bursar's office or download our fees schedule. Payment accepted via bank deposit, Mobile Money (MTN/Airtel), or in person.

🎖️ Scholarships

Sports talent scholarships and academic merit bursaries are available. Contact the admissions office for eligibility criteria and application deadlines.
